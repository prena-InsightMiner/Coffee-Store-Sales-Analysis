# Coffee-Store-Sales-Analysis
Project Overview
This project demonstrates the end-to-end data analysis workflow for a coffee shop’s sales data — including data cleaning, transformation, and visualization through an interactive dashboard.
The dashboard provides actionable insights into product performance, customer preferences, and sales trends.

🔍 Key Insights
🥇 Top Products
The highest-selling product is Barista Espresso, followed by Brewed Chai Tea. These beverages dominate customer preferences and drive major revenue.

🧃 Beverage Size Preference
The most commonly purchased size is Regular, indicating that customers prefer balanced portions over small or large options.

🏬 Store Performance
Among all locations, Astoria leads in total sales, outperforming Hell’s Kitchen and Lower Manhattan.

🍰 Product Category
The Bakery category contributes the most to overall sales, highlighting the popularity of baked goods alongside beverages.

⏰ Peak Sales Hour
Sales reach their highest point at 10 AM, showing strong morning demand as customers grab their daily coffee fix.

📅 Best Day for Sales
Monday records the highest sales volume of the week, suggesting strong weekday traffic and customer engagement.

📈 Dashboard Components
Top Five Products — Visualizes product-level performance.

Profit by Size — Displays profit distribution across beverage sizes.

Sales by Location — Compares store-level performance.

Category Performance — Breaks down sales by product category.

Hourly Sales — Identifies peak sales hours.

Daily Sales — Shows weekly sales trends.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
